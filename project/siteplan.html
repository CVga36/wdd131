BYU–Idaho • WDD 131 • Week 05

Website Planning Document

A design blueprint for an educational website that helps beginners recognize constellations, plan a night of stargazing, and keep an observation journal.

01 / Site identity

Site Name

Night Sky Notebook

I chose this name because it describes both parts of the website: learning about objects in the night sky and keeping personal notes about what visitors observe. It is friendly, memorable, and appropriate for beginners.

The logo combines a star and a small accent star to represent curiosity and sky observation. A commercial domain name is not required for this course project.

02 / Audience and content

Site Purpose

Night Sky Notebook is an informational website for students, families, and people who want to learn basic astronomy without expensive equipment. It will explain what to look for in the night sky, introduce well-known constellations, and share practical tips for a first stargazing experience.

The project is planned as three connected pages:

  1. Home: Introduction to skywatching, beginner-friendly tips, and featured constellations.
  2. Constellation Guide: Cards containing constellation names, observing seasons, short descriptions, and images; visitors can filter the cards using JavaScript.
  3. Observation Journal: A simple HTML form where visitors record the date, location description, and what they observed. JavaScript will save entries using localStorage.

Planned interactivity: Filtering an array of constellation objects, dynamically updating cards, form validation, and saving observations in the browser. These features will be developed during Week 06.

03 / Visitor questions

Scenarios

These questions represent what a typical visitor may want to know:

  • Scenario 1: “I have never watched the stars before. What should I bring, and how can I get started?”
  • Scenario 2: “Which constellations can I look for during different seasons of the year?”
  • Scenario 3: “Can I record the constellations I found so I can remember them later?”

The Home page will address the first scenario, the Constellation Guide will address the second, and the Observation Journal will address the third.
